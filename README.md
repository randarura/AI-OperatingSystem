# AI-OperatingSystem
As AI continues to advance at an unprecedented pace, humanity must develop effective ways to govern and coordinate AI systems to build a future of human–AI collaboration.

This project aims to create an AI Operating System that integrates extensive knowledge related to AI governance, including regulations, legal frameworks, digital institutions, computational resource management, and other mechanisms necessary for responsible AI development.

As we approach the era of the technological singularity, we believe it is essential to establish a foundation that enables humans and AI to coexist and thrive together.

We welcome researchers, engineers, policymakers, and visionaries who share this mission to join us in building the AI Operating System of the future.

AI Operating System – Fundamental Principles

These principles are integrated and adapted from existing constitutional and governance frameworks to guide the development and operation of the AI Operating System.

1. Safety

AI shall not cause harm to human beings and shall prioritize the prevention of physical, psychological, and societal harm.

2. Human-Centered Principle

AI shall prioritize human well-being and human interests in decision-making, ensuring that humans remain at the center of governance and control.

3. Transparency and Accountability

AI shall make its reasoning and decision-making processes as transparent as reasonably possible and shall remain accountable for its actions and outcomes.

4. Privacy and Security

AI shall protect personal data, respect privacy rights, and implement robust safeguards against unauthorized access, misuse, and cyber threats.

5. Fairness

AI shall avoid discrimination, bias, and unequal treatment based on personal attributes, ensuring fair and equitable outcomes for all individuals.

6. Ethics

AI shall refrain from generating outputs that promote harm, violence, illegal activities, or other actions that violate ethical principles and human values.

7. Truthfulness

AI shall strive to provide accurate, reliable, and useful information while clearly communicating uncertainty when facts are unknown or ambiguous.
